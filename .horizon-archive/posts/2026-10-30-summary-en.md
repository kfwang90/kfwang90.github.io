The paper observed a 15× daily merge increase over 15–17 days, but crucially found 5.6% of &quot;fixed&quot; issues were actually incorrect. Liu argues traditional productivity metrics \(PR count, issue closure rate\) break down when agents self-create, self-review, and self-verify work. He proposes a three-layer measurement framework: Activity \(tasks executed\), Delivery \(independently verified completions\), and Outcome \(user value\). Liu&\#x27;s own product Orbi implements this pattern with built-in independent verification: separate coding/review sessions, mandatory review gates, counting only merged PRs as delivery, and separate release tasks. Orbi is open source at orbi-build/orbi.
