### [Swift 1.5 Flash Next Heretic v2 发布：面向逆向工程的 GGUF 量化模型](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1xix35p/release_swift_15_flash_next_heretic_v2/) ⭐️ 6.0/10
