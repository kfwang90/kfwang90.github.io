### [4B 本地模型 Mica v0.5 用多选题链式规划在 Minecraft 中从零合成下界合金](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1xhw2l0/a_4b_model_made_me_a_netherite_ingot_from_bare/) ⭐️ 7.0/10
